# Demo summary: lower respiratory tract infection

This summary goes with the fictional **Demo-Guideline.pdf** in the same folder. Click any underlined phrase to jump to the place it came from.

## Who it covers

The guidance is for [adults from age 16 admitted with suspected pneumonia](#c1). It does not cover hospital or ventilator-acquired infection.

## Severity

- Use CURB-65. One of the five criteria is [a urea above 7 mmol/L](#c2).
- [A score of 3 or more means inpatient care, with ICU review at 4 to 5](#c3).

## Treatment

- Low severity: [oral amoxicillin](#c4).
- Moderate severity: [IV benzylpenicillin, adding doxycycline when an atypical cause is likely](#c5).
- Narrow treatment once cultures return, as [prolonged broad cover raises the risk of C. difficile](#c6).
- Rituximab: [screen for hepatitis B and give antiviral prophylaxis if surface antigen positive](#c7).
- Review at 48 to 72 hours; [5 days is usually enough](#c8).
